Why Edge Architecture

  • Workers + Durable Objects: On-demand stateful messaging with hibernation when idle
  • D1 + R2: Chat histories and media stored at the edge with ultra-low latency
  • Workers AI + Vectorize: 24/7 AI knowledge RAG for instant and accurate answers
  • One script to embed; dynamic secret admin paths + Turnstile bot protection

Core Challenges

Fragmented SaaS & Runaway Server Costs Are Hurting Your Margins

Tool silos prevent lead follow-through, while always-on WebSocket connections silently inflate bills before visitors even ask a question.

Tool Sprawl & Data Silos

Live chat, CRM, and lead capture operate in disconnected silos. High-value leads vanish in conversation logs, requiring tedious manual copy-pasting.

Idle Connection Taxes

Most chat solutions open WebSockets immediately upon page load. As daily UV surges, idle connections drain bandwidth quotas and inflate server bills.

DevOps Burden & Latency

Self-hosting message queues, databases, and auth requires dedicated engineering time. Global visitors suffer from sluggish round-trips to distant origin servers.

Solution

Full-Funnel Customer Operations on a Single Edge Network

CIZHENYU unifies real-time messaging, AI automation, CRM pipelines, and lead forms into a single edge deployment unit—slashing overhead and complexity.

Connect Only on Engagement

Initial load delivers static greetings and badges in milliseconds. WebSocket connections initiate only when visitors open the chat window. Multi-tab sessions sync in real time.

Agent Inbox & CRM Funnel

Real-time customer lists, intent tags, unread alerts, and one-click conversion into CRM customer profiles. Structured pipeline stages turn conversations into closed deals.

AI Knowledge Base (RAG)

Powered by Cloudflare Vectorize and Workers AI: provide instant, accurate responses when agents are offline or off-duty, with smooth escalation to human teams.

Dynamic Forms · CMS · i18n

Embed custom lead-generation forms on any landing page; publish multilingual content. The chat widget automatically adapts to the visitor's preferred language.

Enterprise Security

Engineered for Production & Multi-Tenant Delivery

Built for reliable enterprise deployments with multi-layer defense against unauthorized scanning, credential stuffing, and data leaks.

Turnstile & Passcode Defense

Media & Privacy Purge
